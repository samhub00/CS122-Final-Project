About Food App

We are Alan and Sam, two guys who love cooking! Like the rest of us, we struggle with knowing what to cook sometimes. Our idea for this website was to create a platform that lets you search for tasty recipes without having to scroll through long pre-recipe writing, or close a bunch of pop-up ads. Along with recipes, FoodApp can show you important statistics of your favorite recipes that can assist you in your choices, keep your macros at the right level, and make sure you are getting the right nutrients! Thank you for using FoodApp, we hope you find great things to cook!
